🏗️ My Engineering Approach

Regardless of the programming language, framework, or technology, my approach to building software follows a simple process:

1. Understand the problem – Identify what needs to be solved.
2. Research and plan – Understand the concepts and choose suitable technologies.
3. Design the solution – Break the problem into manageable components.
4. Build and experiment – Implement, test, and iterate.
5. Debug and improve – Investigate failures and improve the implementation.
6. Verify and document – Test the results and document important decisions.

I believe AI tools can accelerate this process, but they don't replace the need to understand the code, verify outputs, and take responsibility for the final implementation.
